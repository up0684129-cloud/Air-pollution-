Atmospheric Dynamics

Thermal Inversion & Smog Trapping Simulation

Under normal lapse conditions, warm surface air rises and carries pollutants into upper atmospheric layers. During nocturnal radiation cooling or winter high-pressure systems, a warm cap forms above the ground, creating a thermal inversion that seals smog tightly inside valleys and urban basins.

Clinical Epidemiology

Human Anatomical Exposure Pathways

Particulate matter does not remain confined to the respiratory tract. Examine organ-specific pathological cascades induced by chronic particulate exposure.

Pulmonary & Terminal Alveoli

Inhaled PM2.5 and Ozone bypass upper respiratory defenses and settle into the alveolar sacs where oxygen exchange occurs. Macrophages engulf particles but cannot digest elemental carbon, resulting in chronic oxidative stress and cell apoptosis.
